Windows: How to paist your custom signature block into Microsoft Outlook.

  • Open Outlook and Click on the "File" menu. (On the top tool bar).
  • On the bottom left; Click "Options"
  • Within the Options modal window; Select "Mail" on the left side. Then Click the "Signatures" Button on the right.
  • Within the Signatures modal; Click "New" on the right.
  • Name the new Signature "FGCU-Signature" and click "OK"
  • Paist in the signature block from above, and click the "Save" Button.
  • Congratulations you now have an official FGCU email signature block.
